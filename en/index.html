What Veiler is

It never shows up in Photos

What you import is copied into Veiler's own storage and never appears in the Photos app. Hand someone your phone, or show them a picture, and there is nothing of yours sitting in the same grid.

The original stays in Photos. Delete it yourself if you want it hidden.

Locked every time it opens

Opened by passcode or Face ID. Going back to the home screen or switching apps locks it again after 30 seconds by default, and you can set it to lock immediately. While you are away the screen itself is hidden too.

Everything can leave, any time

Whatever you keep here can be taken out again in one go. Deciding to stop using Veiler never means being locked out of your own files. The way out exists before you put anything in.
